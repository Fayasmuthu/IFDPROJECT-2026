ABOUT IFD

Who We Are

Interactive Foundation for the Deaf (IFD) is a registered organization working for the upliftment of Deaf children and adults by imparting Islamic education, sign language training and other essential life skills.

Our mission is to promote accessibility, equal opportunity and Islamic knowledge for the Deaf community across various states of India.

Our Mission

Our Vision

A society where Deaf people are independent, confident and spiritually empowered.
