ABOUT IFD

Interactive Foundation for the Deaf (IFD)

Empowering the Deaf community through Islamic education, Indian Sign Language (ISL), life skills, and community development since 2016.

Interactive Foundation for the Deaf (IFD) is a registered educational and charitable trust dedicated to providing accessible Islamic education, empowering Deaf individuals across India through inclusive learning, sign language accessibility, and community support.

Our Mission

To promote accessibility and equal opportunities for Deaf children and adults in all areas of life, and to establish Islamic study centres across India.

Our Vision

Many Deaf individuals face isolation due to communication barriers and limited access to Islamic education. Our vision is to create an inclusive society where they can learn, grow, and thrive with dignity.

WHY IFD

Why Interactive Foundation for the Deaf?

Many Deaf individuals do not have access to proper Islamic education because of communication barriers.

Most parents, madrasa teachers, and communities are not trained to teach Islam through Indian Sign Language. Deaf children are often isolated from Islamic learning, social activities, and community support.

  • Islamic education through Indian Sign Language (ISL).
  • One of India's first systematic initiatives for Deaf Muslims.
  • Managed by experienced Deaf individuals.
  • Online & Offline classes, counselling, camps, and educational programs.
  • Helping Deaf brothers and sisters connect with their Deen confidently.

IFD aims to create a welcoming environment where Deaf Muslims can learn, grow, and feel supported in society.
